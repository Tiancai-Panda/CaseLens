# damaged-packaging

Inspect an overall package photo and details of seals, tears or crushed areas. Record observations with evidence IDs and unknowns. Read the checklist as needed. Use image crop/resize/rotate tools to clarify details while retaining originals. Inspect the product separately before claiming product damage. Policy, current tool permissions and human approval control actions.
