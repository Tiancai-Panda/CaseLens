# Quantity evidence checklist

Obtain ordered quantity and SKU from an order source, a complete overview of received items, and shipment records showing arrived and in-transit packages. Every input cites stored evidence IDs. Customer counts alone are claims. Unknown pending quantity stays null; it is never assumed zero. A complete_photo flag describes the complete received set, not a cropped subset. The helper does not count visually or validate evidence content; the evidence service binds inputs to validated records.
